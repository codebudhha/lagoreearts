Reproduced from a devotional Tanjore study honouring Goddess Saraswati, this piece carries forward a craft tradition rooted in South Indian temple art — where gold leaf, relief work and rich colour once adorned wooden panels. Our archival reproduction preserves the luminous detail of the original while adapting it for the modern wall.
Printed on 400gsm matte poly-cotton canvas with fade-resistant archival pigment ink.
  • Available in S (16″×20″), M (20″×24″), L (24″×30″), XL (30″×40″)
  • Solid wood float frame — Walnut, Black Oak, or Unframed roll
  • Gallery-wrapped edges, ready to hang with pre-fitted hardware
Dispatched in reinforced corner-protected packaging within 5–7 business days.
  • Complimentary delivery on L & XL canvases, pan-India
  • Wipe gently with a dry, soft cloth — avoid direct moisture
  • Keep away from prolonged direct sunlight to preserve colour depth
